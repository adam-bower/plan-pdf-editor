# Plan PDF Editor

A free desktop PDF editor for plan sets.

- **Markup:** clouds, pen, highlighter, text notes, lines, measure and area with scale calibration.
- **Revision overlay:** compare two revisions of a set in blue and red, with auto-align and 3-point align. The exported overlay PDF opens correctly in Bluebeam.
- **Save a marked-up PDF** that any PDF reader can open.

Everything runs on your computer. There's no account, no server, and nothing is uploaded. Your markups are saved on your machine.

## Installing

The installers aren't code-signed yet, so your computer warns you the first time:

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** drag the app to Applications and open it once; macOS will block it. Then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

## Sharing your markups

Markups stay on the computer where you made them. To send them to someone, use **Save marked-up PDF** (or **Export PDF** in the compare view) and share that file.
